Bid Baaz is developed by The Chepu Games. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your gameplay safe and transparent.

1. Virtual Credits Only (No Real-Money Gambling)

Bid Baaz is a strategy and simulation auction game. All auctions and bids inside the game use 100% virtual credits. The app does not involve real-money betting, gambling, or cash payouts.

2. Information We Collect & Local Storage

  • Saved Game Progress: Your active auction progress, squad selections, and settings are stored locally on your device so you can continue saved games offline.
  • Technical Data: Basic anonymous device and crash diagnostics may be processed by the app platform to ensure smooth performance.

3. Advertisements

To support free access to the game, Bid Baaz may display ads via third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Start.io, or Adsterra). These providers may use anonymous advertising identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

4. Optional Pro Features & Subscriptions

If you choose to unlock optional Pro features or subscriptions, payments are handled securely by authorized payment gateways (such as Razorpay, Cashfree, or the official App Store billing provider). We never access or store your UPI PIN, bank, or card credentials.

5. Children & Under-18 Privacy (DPDP Act & COPPA)

Bid Baaz is safe for players of all ages, including students and users under 18.

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: We do not require account sign-up and do not collect, store, or transmit personal details (such as name, email, phone number, or location) from minors or any players. All game saves remain strictly on your local device.
  • No Public Chat or Real-Money Gambling: There is no user-to-user messaging, media upload, or real-money wagering anywhere in the app.
  • Future Updates: If ads or optional Pro features are introduced later, child-directed / non-personalized ad settings and verifiable parental consent safeguards under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and COPPA will be applied before activation.
